Rating Bounty Board

Every new record rating pays out. Each rating type keeps its own record, and points only pay once: after a drop, nothing is earned until the old record is beaten.

Pay rates: €1 per point up to 1800, €2 for 1801–2000, €3 for 2001–2200, €4 for 2201–2400, €5 above 2400. Data from ratings.fide.com, refreshed on the 2nd of each month.
